# Building Innovation — Brand, Materials & AI Technology

> "We design the future."

## Company Overview
Building Innovation is an American company headquartered in Doral (Miami, FL) and a subsidiary of UNITEC USA Design. In its first year in the North American market, Building Innovation serves 10 countries across the Americas (United States, Mexico, Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Dominican Republic, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, and Venezuela) through two equal strategic business units.

## Strategic Business Units

- **URL:** https://www.building-innovation.us/unidades/building-innovation/
- **Catalog (500+ SKUs / 14 Collections, No Cart/Prices Shown Online):** https://www.building-innovation.us/catalogo/
